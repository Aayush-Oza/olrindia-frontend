Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information on OLR India.

Last updated: September 2026

OLR India ("we", "our", or "us") operates the platform available at olrindia.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

  • Name and email address (via registration or Google Sign-In)
  • Mobile number and address details (optional, provided by you)
  • Location data (only if you grant permission)

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To connect customers with local vendors
  • To send account-related emails (verification, password reset)
  • To improve our platform and services

Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from Google. We do not access any other Google account data.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted passwords, HttpOnly cookies, and HTTPS to protect your data.

Third-Party Services

We use Firebase (Google) for authentication and Resend for transactional emails. These services have their own privacy policies.

Your Rights

You may request deletion or export of your personal data at any time by contacting us at support@olrindia.com.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions, email us at support@olrindia.com.
